# NUS-Capstone-Project-Virtual-Bank-GenAI-Chatbot
## 📄 **Overview**  
This repository contains the presentation slides and report for my capstone project, completed as part of my Master’s degree in Digital Financial Technology at the National University of Singapore. The project focuses on optimizing customer support in the banking sector using Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI), based on my internship experience at a virtual bank.

1. **Presentation Slides**  
   - A concise summary of the key projects and findings.  

2. **Capstone Report**  
   - Detailed documentation of the internship, methodologies, and outcomes.  

---

## 📚 **Key Projects**  
### **1. Customer Service Chatbot**  
- **Objective**: Develop a GenAI-powered chatbot to improve customer service efficiency.  
- **Techniques Used**:  
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for accurate responses.  
  - Robust security and privacy protocols for sensitive banking data.  
- **Results**: Streamlined customer interactions and reduced response times.  

### **2. Chatbot Evaluation Framework**  
- **Objective**: Automate the evaluation of the RAG-based chatbot for performance benchmarking.  
- **Framework Used**: RAGAS (Retrieval-Augmented Generation Automated Scoring).  
- **Features**:  
  - Database of 800 test questions.  
  - Automated insights for iterative improvements.

## 🎓 **Internship Takeaways**  
- **Technical Skills**: Advanced coding practices, GenAI libraries, and MLOps infrastructure.  
- **Soft Skills**: Improved communication and collaboration with stakeholders.  
- **Knowledge Gained**: Understanding the risks and challenges of implementing GenAI in banking.
